PopupLoom popup copy swipe file
24 reusable patterns across 8 popup jobs
Last updated: 16 November 2026
Canonical page: https://popuploom.com/tools/popup-copy-swipe-file.html

These are structural patterns, not tested conversion claims. Replace every
[PLACEHOLDER] with a true fact. PopupLoom has not run an A/B test on every
pattern and does not claim a conversion rate for any of them. Check the
consumer-protection, advertising, privacy and consent rules that apply to
your business before publishing. This file is not legal advice.

================================================================================
WELCOME AND FIRST ORDER
================================================================================

1. First-order welcome with a real code

Headline: Your first order, [PERCENT]% off
Body: Use [CODE] at checkout. One use per customer. Valid until [DATE].
Button: Shop with [CODE]
Fine print: [PERCENT]% off one order. Excludes [EXCLUSIONS]. Cannot be combined with other offers. See [TERMS LINK].

2. New-arrival early access

Headline: First look: [COLLECTION]
Body: New pieces go live [DATE]. Get the early-access link before the public launch.
Button: Send me the early link
Fine print: By subscribing you agree to [PRIVACY NOTICE]. You can unsubscribe at any time.

3. Newsletter welcome without a discount

Headline: One useful email, [CADENCE]
Body: [WHAT THEY GET] — written by [WHO], not a daily sales blast. Read the latest issue before you decide.
Button: Get the next issue
Fine print: [CADENCE]. Unsubscribe in one click. [PRIVACY NOTICE].

================================================================================
EMAIL AND LEAD MAGNETS
================================================================================

4. Checklist lead magnet

Headline: The [N]-point [TOPIC] checklist
Body: A printable checklist for [SPECIFIC TASK]. No account needed — enter your email and we will send the link.
Button: Send the checklist
Fine print: One email with the download and [CADENCE] follow-ups. Unsubscribe at any time. [PRIVACY NOTICE].

5. Template pack

Headline: [N] [FORMAT] templates for [AUDIENCE]
Body: Copy the structure, replace the placeholders and publish. The pack includes [FORMAT 1], [FORMAT 2] and [FORMAT 3].
Button: Get the template pack
Fine print: Delivered by email as [FILE TYPE]. [LICENCE TERMS].

6. Webinar registration

Headline: Live on [DATE]: [TOPIC]
Body: [SPEAKER] walks through [SPECIFIC OUTCOME] in [DURATION]. Bring one question for the Q&A.
Button: Save my seat
Fine print: [DATE] at [TIME] [TIMEZONE]. Replay sent to registrants. [PRIVACY NOTICE].

================================================================================
EXIT INTENT
================================================================================

7. Cart-page reminder (written by the owner)

Headline: Still deciding on [PRODUCT]?
Body: Your [PRODUCT] is waiting in the cart. [SHIPPING / RETURNS / STOCK FACT].
Button: Return to cart
Fine print: [OFFER OR NO OFFER]. [TERMS LINK].

8. Content-page exit offer

Headline: Before you go: [RESOURCE]
Body: [ONE-SENTENCE VALUE]. It is free and takes [TIME] to read.
Button: Get [RESOURCE]
Fine print: [PRIVACY NOTICE]. No purchase required.

9. Exit feedback

Headline: Did this page answer your question?
Body: One click helps us fix the gaps. No email required.
Button: Yes, it answered it
Secondary: Not quite — tell us what is missing
Fine print: Answers are anonymous unless you add an email.

================================================================================
ANNOUNCEMENTS AND OPERATIONS
================================================================================

10. Shipping cutoff

Headline: Order by [DATE/TIME] for [DELIVERY WINDOW]
Body: Orders placed after the cutoff ship on [NEXT SHIP DATE]. [REGION] delivery only.
Button: Shop in-stock items
Fine print: Delivery estimates are not guarantees. [CARRIER / REGION DETAILS].

11. New hours or service area

Headline: We are open [NEW HOURS]
Body: Starting [DATE], [LOCATION/SERVICE] runs [HOURS]. [WHAT CHANGES FOR CUSTOMERS].
Button: See the full schedule
Fine print: Holiday hours may differ. [CONTACT LINK].

12. Maintenance notice

Headline: [SERVICE] is down for maintenance
Body: We are fixing [SPECIFIC ISSUE]. Expected back by [TIME] [TIMEZONE]. Orders already placed are unaffected.
Button: Read the status update
Fine print: Updates posted at [STATUS LINK]. No need to email us unless [CONDITION].

WAITLISTS AND RESTOCKS
================================================================================

13. Back-in-stock alert

Headline: [PRODUCT] is out of stock
Body: Leave your email and we will send one message when it is back. No daily reminders.
Button: Notify me
Fine print: One restock email. No purchase obligation. [PRIVACY NOTICE].

14. New-city waitlist

Headline: [CITY], we are coming
Body: Join the launch list for [SERVICE] in [CITY]. We will email the date and the first available slots.
Button: Join the [CITY] list
Fine print: Launch date is an estimate. [PRIVACY NOTICE].

15. Product launch waitlist

Headline: [PRODUCT] launches [DATE]
Body: Get the launch link and [EARLY ACCESS DETAIL] before the public announcement.
Button: Put me on the list
Fine print: One launch email. [PRIVACY NOTICE]. No payment is taken now.

REVIEWS AND COMMUNITY
================================================================================

16. Review request

Headline: How was [PRODUCT]?
Body: A two-minute review helps the next customer choose. We read every response.
Button: Leave a review
Fine print: Reviews are published with your display name. [REVIEW POLICY LINK].

17. Case-study invitation

Headline: Would you share how you use [PRODUCT]?
Body: We are looking for [ROLE] to feature in a short case study. We will ask before publishing anything.
Button: Tell us about your setup
Fine print: Participation is optional. [PRIVACY NOTICE]. No payment is implied.

18. Community invitation

Headline: Join [COMMUNITY] for [AUDIENCE]
Body: [WHAT HAPPENS THERE] — [CADENCE] and [RULES]. Read the pinned post before you introduce yourself.
Button: Open the community
Fine print: [PLATFORM] account required. [COMMUNITY RULES LINK].

SEASONAL AND EVENTS
================================================================================

19. Black Friday or Cyber Monday

Headline: [SALE NAME]: [OFFER] until [DATE]
Body: Use [CODE] at checkout. [WHAT IS INCLUDED]. [WHAT IS EXCLUDED].
Button: Shop the sale
Fine print: Ends [DATE] at [TIME] [TIMEZONE]. While stocks last. Cannot be combined with other offers. [TERMS LINK].

20. Holiday shipping deadline

Headline: Last order date for [HOLIDAY] delivery
Body: Order by [DATE/TIME] with [SHIPPING METHOD]. After that, choose [ALTERNATIVE].
Button: See shipping options
Fine print: Delivery estimates depend on the carrier. [REGION] only. [TERMS LINK].

21. Local event or market

Headline: Meet us at [EVENT] on [DATE]
Body: [LOCATION], [TIME]. [WHAT VISITORS CAN DO]. [TICKET / FREE ENTRY DETAIL].
Button: Get the event details
Fine print: [WEATHER / CANCELLATION POLICY]. [ORGANISER LINK].

================================================================================
FEEDBACK AND RESPONSIBLE EXITS
================================================================================

22. One-question feedback

Headline: What stopped you from [ACTION]?
Body: Pick the closest answer. It takes one click and helps us improve this page.
Options: [PRICE] / [CHOICE] / [TRUST] / [SOMETHING ELSE]
Fine print: No email required. Answers are anonymous unless you add one.

23. Beta invitation

Headline: Try [FEATURE] before it is public
Body: We are testing [WHAT IT DOES] with a small group. Expect rough edges and a feedback form.
Button: Request beta access
Fine print: Access is limited and not guaranteed. [PRIVACY NOTICE]. [SUPPORT LINK].

24. Responsible exit

Headline: Not interested? That is fine.
Body: Close this window and keep reading. If you want [RESOURCE], the link is below.
Button: Close
Secondary: Send me [RESOURCE]
Fine print: No email is required to close. [PRIVACY NOTICE].
