## What this is, and is not

PopupLoom is free and takes no payments, so there is no PopupLoom "sale" to advertise. The campaign is to be the page people find when they search for a Black Friday popup, and to get shop owners to open the studio. Nothing on the site may claim a discount, deadline or result that PopupLoom does not have.
