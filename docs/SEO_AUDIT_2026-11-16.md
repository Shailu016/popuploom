# SEO audit — 16 November 2026

> **Internal record.** Findings and the fixes applied in this pass. Competitor names
> are not used; see `AGENTS.md`.

## Scope and method

Crawled all **42 URLs** in `sitemap.xml` from the live site with a scripted audit:
status, cache policy, title, description, H1/H2, word count, in-content links,
images and alt text, JSON-LD, canonical, robots and byte weight. Built the internal
link graph from in-content links, computed shortest-path depth from the homepage,
and checked the asset bundle separately. No rank-tracking data was used; GSC
exports remain owner-only.

## Findings and fixes applied

1. **Homepage had no `<main>` landmark.** Every other page had one; the homepage
   wrapped its sections directly in `<body>`. Added `<main id="main">` around the
   content sections, leaving the header, footer, modals and scripts outside. This
   is an accessibility and parsing fix, and it also makes the page's word count
   visible to auditors.
2. **Title cannibalisation in the free-tier cluster.** The money page and the guide
   both targeted “free popup builders without pageview limits” almost verbatim.
   Retitled the guide to **“Popup Pageview Limits: How to Audit a Free Plan”**, with a
   matching H1 (“how to audit a free plan in 20 minutes”) and schema headline. The
   money page keeps the product claim; the checklist page owns “how to choose”; the
   guide now owns the audit intent. The guide URL is unchanged, so no equity is lost.
3. **Guides hub title and H1 did not match.** Title was 66 raw characters and the H1
   was simply “PopupLoom guides”. Replaced the HTML entity with “and”, and changed the
   H1 to “Popup guides for ecommerce, quick commerce and lead generation”.
4. **Two category H1s were weaker than their titles.** `gamified` now says
   “Free spin-to-win and giveaway popup templates”; `seasonal` now says
   “Free Black Friday and seasonal popup templates”.
5. **Four template detail pages had one inbound link each, and one sat at depth 3.**
   Added a “Full guides for four popular templates” block on `/templates.html`, and
   added two sibling links to each detail page's Related pages list. All four are now
   at depth 2 with three or more in-content inbound links.
6. **Guide images were heavy PNGs.** Converted all 12 guide screenshots to WebP at
   quality 82: **2,161 KiB → 486 KiB (−77%)**, with 11 in-page references switched to
   `.webp`. PNG originals remain for the Open Graph image and JSON-LD `image` arrays.
   Width/height attributes and lazy loading were already present.
7. **Most informational pages had no visible author and no schema author.** Added a
   byline (“By Shailu, founder of PopupLoom · Last updated 16 November 2026”) to 29
   pages, plus `author` (Person) and `dateModified` on the corresponding Article,
   TechArticle, CollectionPage and WebApplication nodes. The comparison and tool pages
   already had bylines.
8. **Five template category hubs were under 500 words.** Added genuinely
   category-specific guidance, not boilerplate:
   - video: autoplay and consent behaviour, embed loading, captions, clip length;
   - gamified: prize rules, odds disclosure, consent separation, fulfilment;
   - seasonal: publish/retire timing, one offer per visitor, expired-code cleanup;
   - social-proof: evidence for claims, permission, testimonial context;
   - age gates: self-declaration limits, no “verified” claim, jurisdiction caveats.
   Word counts now 545–586, up from 409–479.
9. **Homepage did not link to About or Security in its main content.** Added both to
   the FAQ intro so the trust pages are reachable in one hop from the body copy.

## Remaining issues, in priority order

1. **Indexation is still the binding constraint.** GSC showed 13 indexed / 48 not
   indexed at the last owner export. Nothing in this pass changes that; the sitemap
   and IndexNow submissions are done, but manual indexing requests and a fresh export
   need the owner account.
2. **No external links or mentions.** The site has zero referring domains. Content
   and technical work cannot substitute for distribution; directory listings and
   outreach remain owner-only.
3. **Template category depth.** 545–586 words is acceptable for a hub, not a
   destination. The next content wave should be individual template pages (8–10 per
   wave) rather than more hub copy.
4. **Google Fonts is a render-blocking third-party stylesheet** with four families.
   `display=swap` and preconnect are in place. Self-hosting a subset would remove the
   third-party request, but it is a visual-risk change and was not made here.
5. **Press and Security are footer-discovered.** Security is now linked from the
   homepage body; Press still has one in-content inbound link. Acceptable for a press
   kit, but a link from About would tidy it.
6. **Home HTML is 50 KiB.** Mostly inline SVG and content, not blocking. Worth
   revisiting only if field CWV data shows a problem.

## Limitations

- Audited HTML and assets, not field performance. Core Web Vitals need the GSC report
  or PageSpeed Insights, and the quota was exhausted earlier.
- Link depth is computed from in-content links; header and footer links make the real
  crawl shallower.
- No ranking, traffic or conversion claim is made. Dates in bylines reflect actual
  edits on 16 November 2026.
