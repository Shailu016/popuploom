Free to edit and install. No pageview cap, no trial and no credit card. Template behavior checked on 14 November 2026.

Spin-to-win popups earn attention, but they do not fix a weak offer. The Spin to Win template combines a segmented wheel, a prize layout and a one-field entry. It is a good fit for a real discount, giveaway or launch moment; it is not a certified draw or a regulated lottery platform.

The wheel uses a random display result. It does not enforce odds, verify eligibility, detect duplicate entries or fulfill prizes. Those are the owner’s responsibility, and they should be written down before the popup goes live.

Why this template works

  • The wheel gives the visitor a reason to interact instead of dismissing immediately.
  • A single email field keeps the entry short after the spin.
  • The prize layout makes the result visible and gives the owner a place to explain the terms.
  • The template is compact and works as a modal on desktop and mobile.

Use it when

  • You have a real prize, discount or bundle and clear terms.
  • You can publish eligibility, start/end dates, odds and how winners are chosen.
  • You have a plan to contact winners and deliver prizes.
  • You have checked the promotion rules in the jurisdictions you target.

Set it up

  1. Open the Spin to Win template.
  2. Replace every segment label with a real prize and publish the corresponding terms.
  3. State eligibility, start and end dates, odds and how winners are chosen.
  4. Do not require a purchase if your rules say no purchase is necessary.
  5. Set a frequency that prevents repeat spins from the same visitor.
  6. Save, install, and test one entry. Confirm it appears in the lead inbox.

What to measure

  • Entry rate versus a plain email popup.
  • Duplicate and bot submissions.
  • Prize fulfillment cost and winner response rate.
  • Complaint rate and promotion-rule questions.

Common mistakes

  • Running a giveaway without published rules.
  • Implying odds or a draw that the product does not enforce.
  • Requiring a purchase when the rules say no purchase is necessary.
  • Collecting entries with no plan to contact or fulfill winners.

Common questions

Is the spin result random?

The template uses a random display result. It is not a certified or audited draw, and it does not enforce odds or eligibility.

Can I run a giveaway with this template?

You can collect entries. The rules, eligibility, draw, winner verification and prize fulfillment are your responsibility.

Does PopupLoom verify entries or detect bots?

No. It stores the submitted response; it does not detect duplicate entries, bots or fraud.

Is the template free?

Yes, with no pageview cap. A free account is required to save and install.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. The wheel and entry form are responsive. Test the tap targets and the result screen on a small screen.
