Free seasonal popup templates

Free to edit and install. No pageview cap, no trial and no credit card.

Seasonal campaigns have a short window and a clear reason to act. These three templates cover Black Friday, Cyber Monday and a winter holiday offer. Change the dates, code and terms before publishing.

A seasonal template is not a license to fake urgency. If the sale ends on Sunday, say Sunday and honor it. If the code is limited, state the limit.

Who these templates are for

  • Stores running a real Black Friday, Cyber Monday or holiday promotion.
  • Teams that need a campaign page and popup that match.
  • Shops that can update the offer when the window closes.
  • Marketers who will measure the campaign, not just launch it.

How to choose and use one

  • Set the real start and end date, including timezone.
  • Use a code you have tested in the store.
  • Make the mobile layout legible; seasonal art can crowd small screens.
  • Remove the popup when the promotion ends.
  • Do not reuse last year's deadline or stock claim.

What to measure

  • Code redemption during the real campaign window.
  • Revenue and conversion on mobile versus desktop.
  • Click-through to the campaign page.
  • Whether the popup was removed when the sale ended.

Common mistakes

  • Reusing last year's deadline or stock claim.
  • Publishing a code that was not tested.
  • Letting seasonal art crowd a small screen.
  • Leaving the popup live after the promotion.

Common questions

Can I reuse the template next year?

Yes, but update the dates, code, terms and art. Do not leave an expired deadline live.

Do the countdowns enforce the deadline?

No. They display the date you set. The owner must honor it.

Can I use these on Shopify or WordPress?

Yes, where custom HTML or JavaScript is allowed. See the platform guides.

Are the templates free?

Yes, with no pageview cap.
