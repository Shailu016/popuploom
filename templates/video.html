Free video popup template

By Shailu, founder of PopupLoom · Last updated 16 November 2026

Free to edit and install. No pageview cap, no trial and no credit card.

A video popup is a lightweight way to show a demo, a launch film or a tutorial without sending the visitor to another page. This template embeds YouTube or Vimeo inside a modal and opens it from a call to action.

Video is expensive attention. Keep the clip short, caption it and do not autoplay with sound. The popup is a container; the video provider's privacy and consent rules still apply.

Who these templates are for

  • Product teams with a 30–90 second demo.
  • Course creators previewing a lesson.
  • Launch pages with a founder or product film.
  • Sites that already host video on YouTube or Vimeo.

How to choose and use one

  • Use a short clip and put the value in the first five seconds.
  • Add captions; many visitors watch without sound.
  • Do not autoplay with sound.
  • Check the video provider's privacy settings and consent requirements.
  • Provide a text alternative for visitors who cannot or will not watch.

What to measure

  • Play rate after the popup opens.
  • Completion rate at 25%, 50% and 75%.
  • CTA clicks after the video.
  • Mobile data and load impact.

Common mistakes

  • Autoplaying with sound.
  • Using a long clip with the value buried at the end.
  • Skipping captions.
  • Ignoring the video provider's privacy and consent settings.

Video setup that avoids the usual problems

Browsers block autoplay with sound, so a popup that starts unmuted will either stay paused or be muted by the browser. Start muted, or require a click before playback. Load the player only when the popup opens, so the page is not paying for an embed nobody watches.

Use the provider's privacy-enhanced embed and check what cookies it sets; a video player is a third-party script with its own consent implications. Add captions and a short text fallback, because the embed can be blocked by consent tools or corporate networks. Keep the clip to 60–90 seconds for a launch or demo; if the story needs longer, link to a page instead of forcing it into a modal.

Common questions

Which video providers are supported?

The template is designed for YouTube and Vimeo embeds. The site's CSP allows the standard YouTube, YouTube-nocookie and Vimeo players.

Can I autoplay the video?

You can configure the embed, but autoplay with sound is blocked by most browsers and is a poor experience. Prefer a click-to-play video.

Does the video popup slow my site?

The player loads when the popup opens, not on page load, which keeps the initial page lighter. Test on mobile.

Are the templates free?

Yes, with no pageview cap.
