Free feedback and loyalty popup templates

By Shailu, founder of PopupLoom · Last updated 16 November 2026

Free to edit and install. No pageview cap, no trial and no credit card.

Most popups ask for something. These eight templates ask for something small: a mood check, a one-question micro-commitment, a referral, a review or a birthday signup. The goal is a real signal you can act on, not a vanity metric.

Keep the ask proportional to the relationship. A first-time visitor is not ready for a five-field survey; a returning customer may be. The same template can be used at different moments with different triggers.

Email signup · Discounts · Exit intent · Announcements · Engagement · Trust · Age gates · Gamified · Seasonal · Video

Who these templates are for

  • SaaS and content sites that want lightweight feedback.
  • Local businesses collecting reviews or birthday-list signups.
  • Stores running a referral or loyalty program with real terms.
  • Teams that will follow up on the response.

How to choose and use one

  • Ask one question per popup.
  • Make the options mutually exclusive and easy to tap.
  • Tell the visitor what happens next.
  • Do not promise points, rewards or a reply you cannot deliver.
  • Use once-per-visitor frequency for feedback prompts.

What to measure

  • Response rate and completion rate, separately.
  • Follow-up conversion: what happened after the response.
  • Review or referral volume that was actually published.
  • Repeat-prompt annoyance and close rate.

Common mistakes

  • Turning a one-question prompt into a survey.
  • Asking for feedback and never following up.
  • Promising points or rewards the owner cannot fulfill.
  • Showing the prompt on every page view.

Common questions

Do these templates send email or SMS?

No. PopupLoom captures the response; it does not send marketing email, SMS or push notifications.

Can I run a loyalty or referral program?

The templates provide the signup layout. The program terms, rewards and fulfillment are the owner's responsibility.

Can I collect reviews?

You can ask and store the response. Publishing the review and verifying the customer are your responsibility.

Are the templates free?

Yes, with no pageview cap. A free account is required to save and install.
