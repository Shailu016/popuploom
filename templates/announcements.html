Announcement bars and popups are for information a visitor needs now: a shipping threshold, a delay, holiday hours, a launch or an event. They work best when they are short, specific and easy to dismiss.

These six templates cover top and bottom bars, a launch countdown, an event invitation and operational notices. Keep the message factual; a shipping promise or closure date is an operational commitment, not marketing copy.

Who these templates are for

  • Stores with a real shipping threshold, delay or holiday schedule.
  • Teams launching a product or opening registrations.
  • Event organizers sharing a date and location.
  • Sites that need a dismissible notice without a plugin.

How to choose and use one

  • Put the specific fact first: threshold, date or change.
  • Link to the page that explains the details.
  • Keep the bar to one line on mobile.
  • Use a frequency that matches the notice; a permanent shipping threshold can show every load, a launch countdown should not.
  • Remove the notice when the fact changes.

What to measure

  • Click-through to the details page.
  • Dismiss rate, which shows whether the notice is useful.
  • Support tickets about the same shipping or hours question.
  • Mobile overlap with a fixed site header.

Common mistakes

  • Covering navigation or the first line of content.
  • Leaving a stale notice after the fact changes.
  • Writing a vague notice with no date or link.
  • Using a launch countdown with no real launch time.

Common questions

Will an announcement bar cover my navigation?

Top and bottom bars are fixed-position layouts. Test on mobile and add spacing if your theme's header is also fixed.

Can I schedule a launch countdown?

The countdown is a display layout. Set the target date and time in the studio; the owner is responsible for the launch time.

Can I use these on any website?

Yes, if the site allows custom HTML or JavaScript. Inside an iframe, the popup cannot extend beyond the frame.

Are the templates free?

Yes. No pageview cap; a free account is required to save and install.
