Free email signup popup template

Free to edit and install. No pageview cap, no trial and no credit card. Template behavior checked on 14 November 2026.

An email signup popup should ask for one thing and explain why it is worth it. The Lead Magnet Shelf template uses a corner slide-in, one email field and a short content-upgrade promise. It is less intrusive than a fullscreen modal and works well on content sites, course pages and newsletters.

The template is a layout, not a promise. PopupLoom stores the response in your lead inbox and can export it as CSV. It does not send the file, the email or the follow-up; you deliver those with your own email tool.

Why this template works

  • The corner slide-in keeps the page readable while still being visible.
  • One email field lowers friction compared with a name, phone and company form.
  • The content-upgrade framing gives the visitor a specific reason to subscribe.
  • The layout is easy to test at a delay or scroll depth on mobile and desktop.

Use it when

  • You have a checklist, guide, template or mini-course to give away.
  • You want newsletter signups without a fullscreen interruption.
  • You can deliver the promised file or follow-up promptly.
  • You will honor the consent language and privacy notice you publish.

Set it up

  1. Open the Lead Magnet Shelf template.
  2. Replace the heading and body with the exact thing the visitor gets and when.
  3. Keep one email field unless you have a specific reason for more.
  4. Use a delay or scroll-depth trigger rather than an immediate page-load popup.
  5. Set once-per-day or once-per-visitor frequency.
  6. Save, install, submit a test response, and confirm it lands in the lead inbox.

What to measure

  • Signup rate: submits divided by times the popup was shown.
  • Validation errors, which usually mean the field or the promise is unclear.
  • Lead quality: how many new addresses open the first follow-up.
  • Unsubscribe and complaint rate after the first send.

Common mistakes

  • Promising a file, code or reply nobody will deliver.
  • Adding name, phone and company fields to a first visit.
  • Hiding the consent or privacy link in tiny fine print.
  • Showing the prompt on every page view.

Common questions

Is this email signup template free?

Yes. You can edit and install it without a pageview cap. A free account is required to save the popup and get its install code.

Where do the emails go?

They appear in your PopupLoom lead inbox, tied to the saved widget. You can export them as CSV. An optional signed server webhook can forward them to another tool.

Does PopupLoom send the follow-up email?

No. It captures the response; email delivery and automation are handled by your email provider.

Can I collect emails without a plugin?

Yes, if your site allows custom HTML or JavaScript. Paste the two-line install code before the closing body tag.

Do I need a consent checkbox?

PopupLoom forms do not render a separate consent checkbox. The owner is responsible for the consent language and lawful basis required in their jurisdiction.
