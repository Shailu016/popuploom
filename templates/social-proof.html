Free to edit and install. No pageview cap, no trial and no credit card.

Trust notices only work when they are true. These three templates give you a layout for a guarantee, a secure-checkout explanation and an activity toast. The copy, evidence and claims must come from the owner.

Demo names, times and badges are sample data. Do not publish a fake review, a fake purchase notification or a security claim you cannot substantiate.

Who these templates are for

  • Stores with a real return, guarantee or support policy.
  • Checkout flows that need a plain-language security note.
  • Sites with verified customer activity they can show truthfully.
  • Teams that can keep the claim current.

How to choose and use one

  • Write the exact policy, not a vague “100% safe” badge.
  • Link to the page with the full terms.
  • Remove sample names and times before publishing.
  • Do not imply a certification, audit or payment security standard you do not hold.
  • Update the notice when the policy changes.

What to measure

  • Click-through to the full policy or terms.
  • Support questions that the notice was meant to prevent.
  • Conversion delta before and after the notice.
  • Whether every claim is still true this month.

Common mistakes

  • Publishing sample reviews or names as if they were real.
  • Using a vague badge like “100% safe” with no evidence.
  • Implying a certification, audit or payment standard you do not hold.
  • Leaving an expired guarantee live.

Common questions

Can I use the proof toast with sample data?

No. The sample names and times are demonstration data. Replace them with real, owner-verified activity or remove the template.

Does the secure-checkout template make my site PCI compliant?

No. It is a layout for facts you supply. Compliance depends on your payment provider and your own setup.

Can I claim a guarantee?

Only if you can honor it. State the conditions and link to the full policy.

Are the templates free?

Yes, with no pageview cap.
