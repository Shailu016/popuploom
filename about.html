About PopupLoom

PopupLoom is a free website popup builder. It gives site owners a visual editor, editable starting templates, hosted widget configuration, optional lead capture and CSV export. Popups can be installed on a website where custom HTML or JavaScript is allowed.

Who operates PopupLoom

PopupLoom is an independent product operated by Shailu. It is not a bank, payment processor, retailer or marketplace. The site does not sell products, take payments or ask visitors for card details.

Contact: support@popuploom.com · Security: security@popuploom.com

About the templates

The template gallery contains editable examples for common marketing goals: email signups, discounts, announcements, age gates, giveaways and countdowns. Template names and demo copy are starting points. They are not live offers, and no purchase, prize or discount is promised by PopupLoom. Site owners are responsible for the claims, offers and consent language they publish.

How the product is built

PopupLoom is a small, independently run tool rather than a venture-backed platform. The editor, runtime, hosted configuration and lead inbox are built and maintained in-house. The product is intentionally narrow: one popup runtime, a visual editor, saved configuration and optional lead capture. It does not try to be an email platform, a CRM, a cart-recovery suite or an A/B testing platform.

The site is served from Cloudflare Workers with D1 for accounts, widgets and responses. The Express server in the repository is a development and parity mirror, not a second production deployment. Both runtimes share the same validation, auth and webhook modules so behavior does not drift silently.

Editorial and testing approach

Guides are written from the behavior of the current build and are dated when the behavior was verified. Browser, platform and third-party behavior can change, so each guide says to test on your own site. Template previews are original designs; sample names, times and offers are labelled demonstration data and must be replaced before publishing.

PopupLoom does not claim an independent security certification, a penetration test, a compliance audit or a conversion guarantee. The security page lists the controls that exist and the scope that has not been independently assessed. If you find an error in a guide or a template, email support@popuploom.com and we will correct it.

Data and security

Captured responses belong to the account that owns the popup. Owners can review and export them from the lead inbox. Read the privacy and data handling page for storage, browser storage, webhooks and retention details.

What PopupLoom never asks for. PopupLoom never asks for payment card numbers, bank credentials, one-time codes, or your Google password. Sign-in uses PopupLoom's own login or Google Identity Services. The site does not sell products, take payments or act as a bank, marketplace or delivery service.

Security reports and false-positive blocklist questions are welcome at the contact address above. Response times vary and are not guaranteed.
