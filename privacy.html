Privacy and data handling

PopupLoom stores account email addresses, profile information and password hashes, saved popup configurations, submitted form responses, and aggregate popup view counts. Hosted data is processed using Cloudflare Workers and D1.

Browser storage

The studio stores session tokens and guest drafts in your browser. Embedded popups store only dismissal and frequency preferences on the visitor's browser; submitted form responses are not written to browser storage by the current runtime. Older runtime versions may have cached recent responses locally, and the current runtime removes those old pfsubs.* entries when it loads. Clearing browser storage removes local drafts and signs you out; it does not delete hosted records.

Form responses and integrations

Responses are available to the popup owner through the authenticated lead inbox. Website owners should collect only necessary information and explain their purpose to visitors. Configured webhook destinations receive submitted data, either from the visitor's browser for the optional in-popup browser webhook or from our server for a signed Lead inbox webhook. Third-party services have their own privacy practices. In-popup webhook URLs are public in runtime configuration; server-side webhook URLs and signing secrets are visible only to the signed-in owner. Never put credentials in a public webhook URL.

Advertising

PopupLoom loads Google AdSense on public content pages: the homepage, template catalog, FAQ, press, security, about and guides. Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers and IP-derived data to serve, measure and personalise ads. A Google-certified consent management platform (Google Privacy & messaging) is used to collect consent before personalised ads are served to visitors in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, and visitors can change or withdraw their choices through that platform. US state opt-out choices are provided through the same Google Privacy & messaging tooling where applicable. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google Ad Settings. Advertising is not placed in the editor, account pages, lead inbox, password-reset or migration pages, exports, or customer popup embeds.

External resources

Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party. Pages and templates may request configured remote images or embedded video providers. Those providers receive network requests when their content loads. Google sign-in, when configured, shares the verified identity needed to sign in. Password reset and signup verification links are delivered by Resend when that service is configured; Resend receives the recipient address and the message. PopupLoom stores only a hash of each reset token, and the reset page keeps the token in the URL fragment instead of browser storage.

Retention and control

Saved data remains until deleted. Deleting a popup also deletes its hosted responses and webhook configuration. Account deletion is available in Your account after identity verification and removes the account's widgets, submissions, webhook endpoints, delivery logs, reset tokens and signup verification tokens. Export leads before deletion. Provider backups may remain for their backup retention period. Guest drafts stay in the browser until removed.

Retention details: lead responses remain until the owner deletes the popup or account; webhook delivery history keeps the latest 100 entries per popup; rate-limit counters expire after their short windows; password-reset tokens expire after 30 minutes and are removed during cleanup; signup verification tokens expire after 24 hours and are removed during cleanup; revoked-session records remain until their token expiry. Popup dismissal and frequency preferences remain in the visitor's browser until the visitor clears storage. This page describes current technical handling; it does not promise anonymization or delivery by external services.

Security incidents

Security reports and suspected incidents are triaged by severity. If an incident affects personal data, PopupLoom will contain the issue, preserve evidence, rotate affected credentials and notify affected owners where required. See the security and responsible disclosure page for the reporting channel and current scope.

Privacy questions: support@popuploom.com or use the contact page.
