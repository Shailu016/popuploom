Privacy and data handling

PopupLoom stores account email addresses, profile information and password hashes, saved popup configurations, submitted form responses, and aggregate popup view counts. Hosted data is processed using Cloudflare Workers and D1.

Browser storage

The studio stores session tokens and guest drafts in your browser. Embedded popups store dismissal/frequency preferences and may store recent submitted responses locally. Clearing browser storage removes local drafts and signs you out; it does not delete hosted records.

Form responses and integrations

Responses are available to the popup owner through the authenticated lead inbox. Website owners should collect only necessary information and explain their purpose to visitors. Configured webhook destinations receive submitted data, either from the visitor's browser for the optional in-popup browser webhook or from our server for a signed Lead inbox webhook. Third-party services have their own privacy practices. In-popup webhook URLs are public in runtime configuration; server-side webhook URLs and signing secrets are visible only to the signed-in owner. Never put credentials in a public webhook URL.

Advertising (planned, not active)

PopupLoom does not currently load Google AdSense or any other advertising script, and does not currently set advertising cookies. If advertising is enabled later, it will be limited to selected public guide pages. Google and its partners may then use cookies, device identifiers and IP-derived data to serve, measure and personalise ads. Where required, a Google-certified consent management platform will be used before personalised ads are served to visitors in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, and visitors will be able to change or withdraw their choices through that platform. US state privacy choices will be assessed against the actual audience and advertising configuration; this page does not claim that no state obligations apply. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google Ad Settings. Advertising will not be placed in the editor, account pages, lead inbox, password-reset or migration pages, exports, or customer popup embeds.

External resources

Pages and templates may request Google Fonts and configured remote images or embedded video providers. Those providers receive network requests when their content loads. Google sign-in, when configured, shares the verified identity needed to sign in. Password reset links are delivered by Resend when that service is configured; Resend receives the recipient address and the reset message. PopupLoom stores only a hash of each reset token, and the reset page keeps the token in the URL fragment instead of browser storage.

Retention and control

Saved data remains until deleted. Deleting a popup also deletes its hosted responses. Export leads before deletion. Account deletion is available in Your account after identity verification. Provider backups may remain for their backup retention period. Guest drafts stay in the browser until removed. This page describes current technical handling; it does not promise anonymization, a fixed retention period, or delivery by external services.
