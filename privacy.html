PopupLoom

Privacy and data handling

PopupLoom stores account email addresses, profile information and password hashes, saved popup configurations, submitted form responses, and aggregate popup view counts. Hosted data is processed using Cloudflare Workers and D1.

Browser storage

The studio stores session tokens and guest drafts in your browser. Embedded popups store dismissal/frequency preferences and may store recent submitted responses locally. Clearing browser storage removes local drafts and signs you out; it does not delete hosted records.

Form responses and integrations

Responses are available to the popup owner through the authenticated lead inbox. Website owners should collect only necessary information and explain their purpose to visitors. Configured webhook destinations receive submitted data, either from the visitor's browser for the optional in-popup webhook or from our server for a lead inbox webhook. Third-party services have their own privacy practices. In-popup webhook URLs are public in runtime configuration; server-side webhook URLs and signing secrets are visible only to the signed-in owner. Never put credentials in a public webhook URL.

External resources

Pages and templates may request Google Fonts and configured remote images or embedded video providers. Those providers receive network requests when their content loads. Google sign-in, when configured, shares the verified identity needed to sign in. Password reset links are delivered by Resend when that service is configured; Resend receives the recipient address and the reset message. PopupLoom stores only a hash of each reset token, and the reset page keeps the token in the URL fragment instead of browser storage.

Retention and control

Saved data remains until deleted. Deleting a popup also deletes its hosted responses. Export leads before deletion. Account deletion is available in Your account after identity verification. Provider backups may remain for their backup retention period. Guest drafts stay in the browser until removed. This page describes current technical handling; it does not promise anonymization, a fixed retention period, or delivery by external services.
