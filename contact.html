We aim to reply within two business days. PopupLoom is an independent product operated remotely; there is no walk-in office or phone support line.

What to include

  • Product support: the popup id (starts with wan_), the page where it is installed, and what you expected to happen.
  • Privacy or data requests: the account email and the action you need. Never send your password.
  • Security reports: the URL, a short description and steps to reproduce. Do not include other people's personal data.
  • Blocklist or false-positive reports: the vendor name, the exact warning text, the URL that was blocked and the date.
  • Abuse reports: the popup URL and widget id. We review reports of spam, impersonation or unlawful content.
