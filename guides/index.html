Popup guides for ecommerce, quick commerce and lead generation

By Shailu, founder of PopupLoom · Last updated 16 November 2026

Practical, original guides for installing popups, collecting responses and diagnosing problems. Product screenshots come from the real PopupLoom build. Platform-specific notes were checked against official documentation on 5 November 2026; we did not have hands-on access to every platform, and plan limits or theme filters can change.

Free tools

Four browser-side tools for the work that comes before and after you install a popup. They use your own numbers or your own facts; none publishes a benchmark conversion rate. The tools hub compares what each one takes and where it stops.

  • Popup ROI calculator — enter visitors, displays per visitor, conversion rate, value per signup and tool cost to see monthly signups, annual value, break-even and free-tier cap pressure.
  • Popup copy swipe file — 24 reusable patterns for welcome offers, email signups, exit intent, restocks, announcements, reviews, events and feedback, with placeholders and an avoid-list.
  • Exit-intent code generator — set sensitivity, delay and cooldown, then copy a zero-dependency desktop snippet. It checks for a fine pointer and never runs on touch screens.
  • Popup copy generator — twelve deterministic copy sets from industry, goal and tone, with placeholders and no conversion claims.

  • Lead generation — lead magnets, webinars, waitlists, demos and exit-intent forms, plus where captured leads actually go.

Free-plan limits and how to check them

Free-tier limits are the part of a popup builder that decides whether “free” survives contact with real traffic. These pages explain the units without naming vendors, so the checks work on any shortlist.

  • Free popup builder with no pageview limits — what a traffic meter is, how pageviews, visitors and impressions differ, and what “no limits” does and does not promise here.
  • How to choose a free popup builder — nine checks for any shortlist: the unit, the reset, feature gates, branding, export and the paid cliff.
  • Auditing a free popup tier — a 20-minute test plan you can run on any vendor's pricing page and trial.
  • WordPress popup without a plugin — child-theme or code-snippet placement when plugins are blocked, with cache and CSP caveats.

What these guides do not claim

They do not claim guaranteed conversions, platform endorsements, verified legal compliance, or hands-on testing on every plan. They do not promise that PopupLoom sends marketing email, SMS, PDFs or calendar invitations. Those require the website owner's own integration. Form responses are saved to the owner's lead inbox; delivery and follow-up are the owner's responsibility.
