They do not claim guaranteed conversions, platform endorsements, verified legal compliance, or hands-on testing on every plan. They do not promise that PopupLoom sends marketing email, SMS, PDFs or calendar invitations. Those require the website owner's own integration. Form responses are saved to the owner's lead inbox; delivery and follow-up are the owner's responsibility.
