Free popup builders without pageview limits: a dated comparison

Vendor pricing pages checked on 14 November 2026. Prices and limits change without notice. This page is a snapshot with source links, not a permanent claim about any vendor. Verify on the vendor's own page before you buy or migrate.

Most “free” popup builders are free until a visitor count. That is a reasonable business model, but it is not the same as free. This page records what each vendor's own pricing page said on the check date, so you can compare the actual gate instead of the headline.

What “no pageview limit” actually means

It means there is no plan counter that stops your popup after a number of impressions. It does not mean infinite resources. PopupLoom still applies:

  • per-visitor IP rate limits on public endpoints,
  • a 128 KB limit on a saved widget configuration,
  • a 16 KB limit on a submitted response,
  • standard abuse protections on the API.

None of those is a monthly view quota. They exist so one site cannot degrade the service for everyone else.

When not to choose PopupLoom

A comparison that only lists advantages is marketing, not research. Choose another tool if you need any of these:

  • Cart-value or product-level targeting. PopupLoom does not read cart contents, cart value, customer accounts or order history.
  • Native email or CRM sync. PopupLoom stores the lead and can call your webhook; it does not send email or sync with Mailchimp, Klaviyo, HubSpot or Shopify Customers.
  • A/B testing with statistical reporting. Not included.
  • Team roles, approvals or SSO. Not included.
  • Checkout extensions. A theme or footer install does not cover platform checkout steps.
  • An enterprise support contract or SLA. Not offered.

Free popup builder FAQ

Which free popup builders have no pageview limit?

Of the vendors checked on 14 November 2026, PopupLoom advertises no pageview cap. Poptin publishes 1,000 visitors/month, Popupsmart 5,000 pageviews/month and Elfsight 200 widget views/month. OptinMonster, Sleeknote and Wisepops do not advertise a permanent free plan.

Is PopupLoom really free?

Yes. No credit card, trial timer or paid upgrade inside the product. A free account is required to save a widget and get install code.

What is the catch with unlimited views?

No per-view billing, but fair-use rate limits and fixed size limits still apply. They are not a hidden view quota.

When should I choose a paid tool?

When you need cart-value or product targeting, native email/CRM sync, A/B testing, team roles, checkout extensions or an enterprise support contract.
