An email signup popup asks for one thing and offers one reason to give it. The “welcome popup” pattern — shown to a new visitor after they have had a moment to read — usually works better than a popup that blocks the page immediately. PopupLoom builds the form, stores the response and exports it; your email tool sends the follow-up.

What counts as a welcome popup

A welcome popup is not a newsletter archive and not a full-page gate. It is a single, specific invitation: a checklist, a first-order discount, early access, a webinar seat or a waitlist. The visitor should understand the offer and the cost of subscribing in one screen.

  • One field is usually enough. Email, optionally a first name. Every extra field lowers completion.
  • One sentence of value. “Get the 12-point launch checklist” beats “Subscribe to our newsletter”.
  • One privacy link. The form does not render a consent checkbox, so the surrounding page must explain what happens to the address.
  • One frequency rule. Once per day or once ever; not on every page view.

Build the popup in PopupLoom

  1. Open the studio and choose an email signup template, or start blank.
  2. Add a heading, one short paragraph, the form block and a privacy link. Keep the close control visible.
  3. Use an email field. Add a name field only if you will actually personalise with it.
  4. Set the trigger and frequency. Set page targeting to exclude checkout, account and legal pages.
  5. Save, open Publish, and copy the two-line install code.
  6. Install it on your site. Platform steps: WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace and Wix.
  7. Submit a fake response on the live site and confirm it appears in Leads.

Where the email actually goes

PopupLoom stores the response against the saved widget. The signed-in owner can read it in the lead inbox and export CSV. Optional webhooks can forward it:

  • Browser webhook: visible to site visitors. Never put a secret or private CRM key in it.
  • Server-side webhook: HMAC-SHA256 signed, owner-only, with retries and a delivery log. Use this for automation.

PopupLoom does not send the welcome email, run a double opt-in, or sync natively with Mailchimp, Klaviyo or HubSpot. Connect your own tool. If your jurisdiction or provider requires confirmed opt-in, send the confirmation from that tool and only add the address to your list after it is confirmed.

Consent and privacy basics

The form collects personal data. The website owner is the controller of that data, not PopupLoom. Before publishing:

  • Link to a privacy notice that explains what you collect, why, how long you keep it and how to unsubscribe or request deletion.
  • Do not pre-tick consent or hide the purpose in fine print.
  • If you need a separate consent checkbox, add it as a choice/link block or collect consent in your email tool; the built-in form fields do not include one.
  • Do not ask for passwords, card numbers, one-time codes or government IDs in a popup form.
  • Honour unsubscribe and deletion requests in your own systems.

Read the PopupLoom privacy and data handling page for what the product stores and for how long.

Test and measure

  • Test on desktop and phone; the popup must be closable without zooming.
  • Check that the form submits, shows a success state and stores exactly one lead per submission.
  • Use the frequency rule to avoid counting the same visitor repeatedly.
  • Compare two offers, not two colours. The offer is the variable that matters.
  • Watch form completion, not only popup views. A high view count with no submissions is a bad popup.

Email signup popup FAQ

What is a welcome popup?

An email signup popup shown to a new visitor after a delay, scroll or exit signal, offering one specific reason to subscribe.

Does PopupLoom send the welcome email?

No. It stores the response and can forward it to a signed webhook. It does not send marketing email, SMS or automated follow-ups.

Does the form include a consent checkbox?

No. The built-in fields are text, email, phone, number, date and URL. The owner is responsible for the lawful basis, privacy notice and any consent language required.

When should it appear?

Not immediately on first load. A short delay, scroll depth or exit intent usually interrupts less. Exclude checkout, account and legal pages.

Is it free?

Yes. No pageview cap and no paid upgrade inside the product. A free account is required to save and install a popup.
