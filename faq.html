Free plan and account

Is PopupLoom free?

Yes. PopupLoom is a free website popup builder. Designing and previewing a popup needs no account and no payment. A free account is required to save a widget and get its hosted installation code.

Do I need an account to use PopupLoom?

You can open the studio, choose a template and preview a popup as a guest. Guest drafts stay in your browser. To save a popup online, get an install code and collect responses, create a free account.

Is there a credit card, trial or paid upgrade?

No. The product has no credit card step, no trial countdown and no paid upgrade inside the studio. The site does not sell products or take payments.

How many templates and popups are included?

The gallery includes 45 templates, and you can also start from a blank canvas. Templates are starting designs for email signup, discounts, announcements, age gates, giveaways, countdowns, feedback and trust notices.

Editing, triggers and targeting

What popup triggers can I use?

Each widget uses one automatic trigger: page load, a timed delay, scroll depth, scroll to a CSS selector, a click on a CSS selector, or desktop exit intent. Page targeting, device settings and frequency rules are configured separately.

Can I target specific pages and devices?

Yes. Include or exclude page paths with wildcard patterns such as /shop/*, and enable or disable desktop and phone separately. Frequency can be every load, once per day, once per week, once ever, or until dismissed with a rest day.

Installation and platforms

How do I install a popup on my website?

Save the popup, open Publish, and copy the two-line snippet: a widget element with your saved widget id and a script tag loading https://popuploom.com/popuploom.js. Paste both before the closing body tag of your site.

Does PopupLoom work with WordPress?

Yes, where the site allows custom HTML or JavaScript. You can use a footer-code plugin, a theme hook or a Custom HTML block. Caching, script optimisation and security plugins can delay or block the runtime, so test the published page.

Does PopupLoom work with Shopify?

You can install the embed in Shopify theme code, usually layout/theme.liquid before the closing body tag. It covers the storefront, not the checkout: Shopify checkout has separate rules and PopupLoom does not provide a checkout extension.

Does PopupLoom work with Webflow?

Yes, where your Webflow plan permits custom code. Add the snippet site-wide in custom code settings or on a specific page, publish, and test the live domain. Plan permissions and publishing steps vary.

Can I use PopupLoom on Wix, Squarespace or Google Sites?

Sometimes. It depends on whether your plan and editor allow custom HTML or JavaScript in the page body. Editor previews and embed-only areas can trap a widget inside an iframe, which limits fixed-position popups. Test the published page, not only the editor.

Do PopupLoom popups work on mobile?

Yes. Popups render on phones, and desktop and phone can be enabled separately. Exit intent is desktop only. Test the close button, text size and form on a real phone before publishing.

Leads, exports and integrations

Where are captured leads stored?

Form submissions are stored against the saved widget that produced them. Only the signed-in widget owner can read them, through the authenticated lead inbox. Deleting the widget also deletes its hosted responses.

Can I export leads to CSV?

Yes. The lead inbox includes a CSV export for all saved responses owned by your account. The export is a snapshot, not a live CRM sync, so re-export after new submissions arrive.

Does PopupLoom send marketing emails or SMS?

No. PopupLoom stores the response and can forward it to a webhook you configure. Sending a welcome email, PDF, SMS or calendar invitation requires your own email, SMS or automation platform.

Limits and fit

Can a popup show cart contents or recover abandoned carts?

No. PopupLoom does not read cart contents, cart value, customer accounts or order history. You can target cart and product page paths and write copy for that context, but the popup cannot display live cart items or send abandoned-cart emails.

Can PopupLoom run inside a native mobile app?

No. PopupLoom is a website widget. It can run on a website or progressive web app where you control the page and can add a script, but it cannot inject messages into a native iOS or Android app, a marketplace listing or a third-party ordering app.

Does PopupLoom offer A/B testing or revenue attribution?

No. The product shows views per popup and stores form responses, and open and submit events can reach the Google Tag Manager data layer when GTM is present. It does not split traffic, calculate statistical significance or attribute revenue.

What are the technical limits?

A saved widget configuration is limited to 128 KB and a visitor response to 16 KB. Public endpoints apply per-visitor IP rate limits. Guest drafts live in browser storage, and a widget cannot escape an iframe it is embedded in.

Can I use PopupLoom for ecommerce, quick commerce or lead generation?

Yes. The studio supports first-order offers, cart-page exit intent, free-shipping bars, back-in-stock forms, serviceability notices, launch bars, app-install links, lead magnets, webinars, waitlists and demo requests. Separate free playbooks cover ecommerce, quick commerce and lead generation.

Does PopupLoom guarantee more conversions?

No. Results depend on your traffic, offer, audience, placement and timing. Test one change at a time and respect visitor preferences. PopupLoom does not publish or promise conversion rates.
