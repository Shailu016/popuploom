Most “free” popup builders are metered. The free plan works until a pageview, visitor or impression cap is reached, and then the popup stops, the plan auto-upgrades, or you are charged for overage. PopupLoom does not meter pageviews or visitors for your popup. The product is free, a free account is required to save and install, and there is no credit-card step.

This page explains what the common limits actually measure, compares the free tiers we could verify from each vendor’s own pricing page, and says plainly when PopupLoom is the wrong choice.

What “no pageview limits” actually means

“Pageview”, “unique visitor” and “campaign impression” are different meters. A plan can look generous on one and expensive on another.

  • Pageview: every page load on your site. A 10-page visit counts as 10.
  • Unique visitor: one person or browser per month, however many pages they read.
  • Campaign impression: every time the popup is shown. A sitewide popup can use one impression per visit even if the visitor reads several pages.
  • Overage: what happens at the cap. Some tools stop the popup, some auto-upgrade the plan, and some charge per extra block of traffic.

PopupLoom has no pageview, visitor or impression meter for the popup itself. Hosting and API rate limits exist to stop abuse, not to bill you for traffic. The trade-off is scope: PopupLoom is a focused popup and lead-capture tool, not a full campaign suite.

The unit matters more than the number

A 5,000-pageview allowance and a 1,000-visitor allowance are not comparable. If a visitor reads five pages, the pageview plan counts five and the visitor plan counts one. A campaign-impression plan counts only when the popup is shown, which can be cheaper on a large site with narrow page targeting and more expensive on a sitewide popup.

Before choosing a free plan, write down four things: the unit, the monthly number, what happens at the cap, and whether the free tier is permanent or a trial. The table above uses each vendor’s own wording as closely as possible.

When PopupLoom is the wrong choice

Be honest about scope. PopupLoom does not do everything a paid campaign suite does:

  • No built-in email or SMS sending, marketing automation or CRM.
  • No A/B testing, multi-step campaign builder, geotargeting or audience segmentation beyond page, device and frequency rules.
  • No cart contents, cart value, customer accounts or order history.
  • No coupon generation, validation or syncing with a store.
  • No native iOS/Android SDK or marketplace listing embed.
  • No managed migration or enterprise support contract.

If those are requirements, choose a tool that has them. If you mainly need a well-designed popup, lead capture and no traffic meter, PopupLoom is a strong fit at zero cost.

Switch without losing leads

  1. Export the leads you already have from your current tool and store them with your records.
  2. Build the replacement popup in the PopupLoom studio. Test it as a guest first.
  3. Save it, open Publish, and copy the two-line install code.
  4. Add the code to your site and pause the old popup. Do not run both on the same page.
  5. Test desktop and mobile, submit one fake response, and confirm it appears in the lead inbox.
  6. Remove the old tool’s code. Export PopupLoom leads as CSV whenever you need them.

There is no automatic lead import. Keep the old CSV; PopupLoom starts collecting new responses after installation.

How to check any builder’s limit yourself

  1. Open the vendor’s pricing page, not a review site.
  2. Find the free tier and write down the unit: pageviews, visitors, impressions, contacts or live popups.
  3. Find the “at the cap” behavior: stop, auto-upgrade or overage charge.
  4. Check whether the free tier is permanent or a trial.
  5. Check the install method: plugin, app or script.
  6. Record the date. Limits change, and a stale comparison is worse than none.

Common questions

Is PopupLoom really free with no pageview limits?

Yes. PopupLoom does not meter pageviews, unique visitors or popup impressions. There is no trial countdown and no paid upgrade inside the product. A free account is required to save a popup and get its install code. Hosting and API rate limits protect the service from abuse.

Do I need a credit card?

No. You can design and preview as a guest, and create a free account without a card to save and install a popup.

What is the catch?

PopupLoom is deliberately narrow. It does not send email or SMS, run A/B tests, read cart data, generate coupons or provide a native app SDK. If you need those, a paid platform may fit better. The comparison below says who each tool is for.

Can I use it on WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace or Wix?

Yes where the platform allows custom HTML or JavaScript. PopupLoom is a two-line script, not a plugin. Platform-specific steps are in the WordPress, Shopify, Webflow and Squarespace/Wix guides.

Where are captured leads stored?

In your PopupLoom lead inbox, tied to the saved widget. You can export them as CSV. An optional signed server webhook can forward them to another tool.

Can I import leads from my old popup tool?

There is no automatic importer. Export the old leads as CSV and keep them with your records, then install PopupLoom for new responses.
