PopupLoom vs OptinMonster: a free alternative with no pageview limits

OptinMonster terms checked on 14 November 2026. Verify current pricing on its site before buying.

OptinMonster is a mature campaign suite with a large template library, advanced targeting, A/B testing and integrations. It is a good product for teams that need those features and can pay for them. This page is for the narrower case: you want a well-designed popup, lead capture and no traffic meter, and you do not need the full suite.

PopupLoom is free and does not meter pageviews, visitors or popup impressions. A free account is required to save and install. It is a two-line script, not a WordPress plugin, and it works on any site that allows custom HTML or JavaScript.

Where OptinMonster is stronger

  • Campaign depth. A/B testing, multi-view campaigns, content locking and campaign scheduling are part of the paid tiers.
  • Targeting. Geolocation, new versus returning visitors, referrer, cookie and query targeting go beyond page and device rules.
  • Integrations and analytics. A broad integration library, revenue attribution and fuller reporting.
  • Templates and support. A very large template library and a mature support organization.
  • WordPress plugin. A native plugin path if you want to manage campaigns inside WordPress.

Where PopupLoom fits

  • No traffic meter. Your popup does not stop because a pageview or impression allowance ran out.
  • Free with no card. No trial countdown and no paid upgrade inside the product.
  • Simple install. Two lines before the closing body tag, with no plugin required.
  • Leads you can take with you. Lead inbox, CSV export and an optional signed server webhook.
  • 45 original templates across email, discounts, exit intent, announcements, engagement, trust, age gates, gamified, seasonal and video.

Who should switch

  • You run a small site or store and only need a few popups.
  • Your OptinMonster impression allowance is the bottleneck, not the feature set.
  • You want a script you can install without a plugin.
  • You are comfortable exporting leads and handling follow-up in your own email tool.

Who should stay

  • You rely on A/B testing, geotargeting, multi-step campaigns or revenue attribution.
  • You need a native WordPress plugin and managed support.
  • You need the broader integration library or enterprise arrangements.

Switch in about ten minutes

  1. Export your OptinMonster leads and keep the CSV.
  2. Build the popup in the PopupLoom studio and test it as a guest.
  3. Save it, open Publish, and copy the two-line install code.
  4. Add the code, pause the OptinMonster campaign, and test one fake submission.
  5. Confirm the response in the lead inbox, then remove the old campaign code.

Common questions

Is PopupLoom a free OptinMonster alternative?

Yes. PopupLoom is free and does not meter pageviews, visitors or popup impressions. It does not include OptinMonster's advanced campaign suite, A/B testing, geotargeting or managed support.

What is OptinMonster's free plan?

OptinMonster's pricing page on 14 November 2026 describes a free trial with 500 campaign impression credits, which auto-upgrades to the paid Plus plan when exceeded. Verify the current terms on OptinMonster's pricing page.

Can I use both tools?

You can, but do not show two popups on the same page. Use one as the primary popup and remove the other, or target them to different pages and audiences.

Does PopupLoom send email?

No. It captures the response and can export it or forward it to a signed webhook. Email delivery and automation are handled by your email tool.

Can I migrate my OptinMonster leads?

Export them from OptinMonster and keep the CSV. PopupLoom has no automatic importer; it collects new responses after installation.

Is PopupLoom affiliated with OptinMonster?

No. OptinMonster is a trademark of Retyp, LLC. PopupLoom is not affiliated with or endorsed by OptinMonster.
