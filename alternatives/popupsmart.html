A free Popupsmart alternative with no pageview limits

Popupsmart terms checked on 14 November 2026. Verify current pricing on its site before buying.

Popupsmart is built for marketers who want an AI-assisted builder, a large template library, Shopify integration and campaign targeting. Its free plan is useful for a single site, but it is metered by pageviews and limited to one live popup when we checked.

PopupLoom is free and does not meter pageviews. It is a smaller, focused tool: 45 templates, six triggers, page/device/frequency targeting, lead inbox, CSV and optional signed webhook. It does not include AI campaign generation, A/B testing or geotargeting.

Where Popupsmart is stronger

  • AI-assisted campaign creation and a library of 500+ templates.
  • Shopify app with cart and product data, audience targeting and revenue tracking.
  • Advanced targeting including geolocation, traffic source, cookies and new versus returning visitors.
  • A/B testing and fuller analytics on paid plans.
  • Email quota and integrations with the major email and CRM tools.

Where PopupLoom fits

  • No pageview cap. Your popup does not stop at 5K pageviews or require an upgrade.
  • Free with no card. No trial timer and no paid upgrade inside the product.
  • No app dependency. Two lines on any site that allows custom HTML/JavaScript.
  • Portable leads. Lead inbox, CSV export and a signed server webhook.
  • 45 templates with clear setup notes and category guidance.

Who should switch

  • Your site is not on Shopify, or you do not need cart-based targeting.
  • You need more than one live popup on the free tier.
  • Your pageviews are near or above 5K per month.
  • You want a focused script and will handle email/CRM elsewhere.

Who should stay

  • You need the Shopify app and cart/product data.
  • You rely on AI campaign generation, geotargeting or A/B testing.
  • You need the larger template library and managed support.

Switch in about ten minutes

  1. Export your Popupsmart leads and keep the CSV.
  2. Build the popup in the PopupLoom studio and test it as a guest.
  3. Save it, open Publish, and copy the two-line install code.
  4. Add the code, pause the Popupsmart campaign, and test one fake submission.
  5. Confirm the response in the lead inbox, then remove the old campaign.

Platform steps: WordPress, Shopify, Webflow and Squarespace and Wix.

Common questions

Is PopupLoom a free Popupsmart alternative?

Yes. PopupLoom is free and does not meter pageviews. Popupsmart's free plan was listed at 5K pageviews per month, one website and one live popup when checked on 14 November 2026.

What is Popupsmart's free plan?

Popupsmart's pricing page on 14 November 2026 listed a free plan with 5K pageviews per month, one website, one live popup and a 100-email monthly quota. Verify the current terms on Popupsmart's pricing page.

Does PopupLoom have a Shopify app?

No. PopupLoom installs as a two-line script. On Shopify, add it to theme code where custom JavaScript is allowed; the Shopify guide explains the limits.

Does PopupLoom have A/B testing or geotargeting?

No. It has page/device/frequency targeting and six triggers, but not A/B testing, geotargeting or audience segmentation.

Can I migrate my Popupsmart leads?

Export them from Popupsmart and keep the CSV. PopupLoom has no automatic importer; it collects new responses after installation.

Is PopupLoom affiliated with Popupsmart?

No. Popupsmart is a trademark of its owner. PopupLoom is not affiliated with or endorsed by Popupsmart.
