PopupLoom vs Popupsmart: a free alternative without pageview limits

By Shailu, founder of PopupLoom Published 13 November 2026 Last updated 16 November 2026 Popupsmart pricing checked 16 November 2026

The short answer. Popupsmart's free plan allows 5,000 pageviews per month, one website and one live popup campaign, with a 100-email monthly quota. Paid plans start at $390 billed annually for 50,000 pageviews and rise to $1,590 for 500,000. Larger accounts are quoted individually.

PopupLoom has no meter and no paid tier. It is also narrower: no A/B testing, no AI targeting, no email sending, no integration library. If you want a popup platform with split testing and a template library, Popupsmart's paid plans are a reasonable answer. This is a pricing-page review, not a hands-on trial.

Popupsmart describes itself as a no-code popup builder for growing websites. Its pricing page sells five allowances together: popup designs, pageviews, website slots, an email quota and team seats. The cheapest plan that fits your pageviews may not fit your website count.

The meter is the part to understand first. Popupsmart counts pageviews, not unique visitors. A reader who opens ten pages counts ten times against the allowance. On a content site or a store with category pages, that is a faster meter than visitor-based plans. PopupLoom does not meter either unit.

This page records what each plan includes, where the free tier stops and how a migration works. Every price is dated and linked so you can recheck it.

What Popupsmart is, and who it is built for

The pricing page presents four connected parts:

  • Popup campaigns. Announcement, email collection, form collection, coupon, cookie consent, recommendation, cart-abandonment, gamification and phone-call campaigns. The page lists more than 500 conversion-optimised designs and a drag-and-drop builder with custom fonts, custom CSS and display effects.
  • Targeting rules. After a delay, exit intent, inactivity, scroll depth, specific pages, new versus returning visitors, traffic source, display frequency, operating system, browser language, cookie value, geolocation and HTML content.
  • Integrations. Mailchimp, Klaviyo, HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Drip, Salesforce, Customer.io, MailerLite, SendGrid, Brevo, Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Zapier and Make, plus local data storage if you do not connect a provider.
  • Plan capacity. Pageviews, websites, live campaigns, email quota and team members, each with a number on every tier. A/B split testing appears from Advanced upward.

That shape suits a marketing team running several campaigns across several sites, especially a Shopify or ecommerce operation that wants coupon, cart and recommendation popups alongside email capture. Popupsmart's footer also lists an agency plan and an enterprise quote path above 500,000 pageviews.

PopupLoom is a single-purpose widget: 45 templates, six triggers, page and device targeting, frequency rules, a lead inbox, CSV export and an optional signed webhook. It does not run campaigns across a portfolio of sites, split-test or send email.

What the free plan actually includes

The free tier is more generous than a trial and stricter than it looks. It is forever-free with no credit card required. On it you get:

  • Unlimited popup creation, one live campaign. This is the detail most people miss. You can design and save as many popups as you like, but only one campaign can be published and running at a time. A welcome popup and an exit-intent popup cannot both run on the free tier.
  • 5,000 pageviews per month. Pageviews, not visitors, and the limit is monthly. A site with 3,000 monthly readers who each view two pages has already used 6,000 pageviews and is over the allowance.
  • One website. A second domain needs Basic, which allows three, or Expert, which allows unlimited.
  • A 100 email quota per month. The page calls this an “Email Quota” and pairs it with pageviews as a monthly quota. Confirm with support whether it counts collected addresses, emails sent or credits.
  • Standard support. Priority support starts on Basic.
  • No team seats and no A/B testing. Seats start at one on Basic; split testing starts on Advanced.

That is usable for one small site running one campaign. It is not a free plan for a store that wants a welcome popup, a cart-rescue popup and a newsletter popup live at once: the one-live-campaign rule binds before the pageview meter does.

What happens at the quota is not answered in the pricing text we captured. The FAQ asks “What happens if my monthly quotas will not enough for pageview or email?” but the answer sits behind an expandable element that did not render in our capture. Ask support whether the popup stops, the account is throttled or an overage is billed before you plan a launch around the free tier.

Where Popupsmart is stronger than PopupLoom

A fair comparison starts with what the other product does better. Popupsmart is stronger on breadth, testing and team workflow.

  • Design library. The page lists more than 500 conversion-optimised designs across many popup types. PopupLoom has 45 templates and a blank canvas.
  • Split testing and analytics. A/B testing starts on Advanced at $690 a year. PopupLoom has no split-testing feature and no campaign analytics screen.
  • Targeting depth. Cookie value targeting, geolocation, HTML content, traffic source, new versus returning visitors and browser language go beyond PopupLoom's six triggers and page rules.
  • Integration library. Direct connections to the major email and CRM platforms, plus Zapier and Make. PopupLoom hands leads over by CSV or a signed webhook and expects you to wire the rest.
  • Multiple sites and seats. Basic covers three websites; Expert covers unlimited sites and four seats. PopupLoom has no site-count concept and no team roles.
  • Ecommerce campaign types. Cart-abandonment reminders, coupon popups, recommendation popups and Meta Pixel lead events are listed as product features.
  • AI targeting. The grid lists a “Smart Mode with AI” that chooses targeting automatically. We did not test it, so verify which plans include it.

Popupsmart also publishes customer stories with conversion figures: Gemplers 5.83% on a welcome popup, BermudAir 13% average, RP Strength 14% across campaigns, Somfy 47.27% on a US cart-abandonment popup and BellaTentazione 4.29%. Those are vendor-published case studies about specific campaigns, not averages to expect, and we did not audit the data behind them.

The page also displays G2 and Capterra badges at 4.5/5. Those are vendor-displayed review scores; we did not verify the review counts or methodology.

Where PopupLoom fits

PopupLoom's advantage is not feature count. It is that the popup has no traffic meter and the data stays portable. For a site that already has email, analytics and a store platform, a pageview allowance is a subscription for capacity you may not need.

  • No traffic meter. No pageview, visitor or impression limit on the popup. Hosting and API rate limits protect the service, but there is no plan to upgrade when traffic grows.
  • Free with no card and no trial timer. A free account is required to save a popup and get its install code; guests can design and preview first.
  • Two-line install. A widget element with the saved widget id plus a script tag. The configuration is hosted, so edits go live without re-pasting the snippet.
  • Portable leads. A lead inbox, CSV export and an optional signed server webhook with HMAC-SHA256, retries and a delivery log. No contacts product is required.
  • Enough for a focused popup. 45 templates, six triggers, page include/exclude rules, device switches, frequency rules, countdowns, copyable codes and form fields.

The trade-offs are real: no A/B testing, no AI targeting, no email sending, no coupon generation, no cart reading, no team seats and no native app SDK. PopupLoom also cannot run where custom scripts are blocked. The installation guide covers the platform requirement, and the WordPress without a plugin guide covers the common blocked-script cases.

Who should switch, and who should stay

Switch to PopupLoom if…

  • You run one or two popups, not a campaign calendar across a portfolio of sites.
  • Your pageviews are near or above 5,000 a month and the meter is the reason you are considering a paid plan.
  • You already use an email provider and do not need Popupsmart's integrations or email quota.
  • You want a simple script, a hosted configuration and no account hierarchy.
  • You are comfortable exporting leads or forwarding them to a webhook.

Stay with Popupsmart if…

  • You need several live campaigns at once and more than one website.
  • You want A/B split testing, campaign analytics or AI-assisted targeting.
  • You rely on cart-abandonment, coupon or recommendation popups for a store.
  • You need team seats, an agency arrangement or direct email and CRM integrations.
  • You are under 5,000 pageviews a month with one campaign and the free plan already fits.

How to migrate from Popupsmart in about half an hour

The migration is mostly bookkeeping plus one rebuild. PopupLoom does not import historical submissions, so keep the export and treat the switch as a clean start.

  1. Export your Popupsmart submissions. If you connect an email or CRM provider, confirm the historical contacts are in that provider too. Keep the export where your team can reach it.
  2. List the campaigns that are live. Note the offer, pages, trigger, frequency and the email quota each one uses. On the free tier only one can run.
  3. Rebuild the most important popup first. Open the PopupLoom studio, pick a template from the catalog and replace every placeholder with a real offer, code and date.
  4. Set the behavior. Choose one trigger, one placement and a frequency rule. A welcome popup and an exit-intent popup should not share settings.
  5. Test as a guest. Submit a fake response, close and reopen the popup, and check desktop and phone widths. The exit-intent guide covers the desktop-only trigger.
  6. Save, publish and install. Copy the two-line snippet from the publish panel and add it before the closing body tag. Platform notes: WordPress, Shopify, Webflow and Squarespace and Wix.
  7. Pause Popupsmart, then verify. Disable the old campaign, load the page in a private window and confirm the response lands in the PopupLoom lead inbox.
  8. Remove the old script. Once the new popup has run a full business cycle, delete the code and cancel if you no longer need its integrations, seats or reporting.

If you want the lead-routing details first, the collect and export leads guide explains the inbox, CSV and webhook options. If the old campaign used cart or coupon data, expect that not to carry over: PopupLoom does not read a cart or generate codes.

How to evaluate Popupsmart's free plan yourself

  • Convert your traffic to pageviews. Pull a month of pageviews, not users, from analytics and compare with 5,000.
  • Count the campaigns you need live. If the answer is more than one, the free tier is already too small regardless of traffic.
  • Find the at-quota behavior. Ask support whether the popup stops, the account is throttled or an overage is billed, and whether the email quota blocks collection separately.
  • Pin down the guarantee. Two different windows are printed on the same page. Get the one that applies to your billing cycle in writing.
  • Check the seats and site count. A plan can fit your pageviews and still fail on websites or team members.
  • Check the export path. Confirm you can export submissions and contacts in a usable format.

Common questions

Is PopupLoom a free Popupsmart alternative?

Yes, with a narrower scope. PopupLoom is free and does not meter pageviews, visitors or popup impressions. It does not include Popupsmart's 500+ designs, A/B split testing, AI targeting, email quota, team seats or integration library.

What is Popupsmart's free plan?

On 16 November 2026 Popupsmart's pricing page listed a free plan with 5,000 pageviews per month, one website, one live popup campaign, unlimited popup creation, a 100 email quota per month and standard support.

Does Popupsmart count pageviews or visitors?

Popupsmart meters pageviews, not unique visitors. Its FAQ asks what the pageview limit is and confirms it is monthly. A reader who loads ten pages therefore counts ten times against the allowance, which makes the 5,000-pageview free tier stricter than a 5,000-visitor tier.

How many popups can I run on Popupsmart's free plan?

The free plan allows unlimited popup creation but one live popup campaign. The distinction matters: you can build and keep as many designs as you like, but only one can be published and running at a time on the free tier.

What happens when Popupsmart's pageview quota runs out?

Popupsmart's FAQ addresses what happens when monthly quotas are not enough, but the answer is not visible in the pricing text we captured. Ask support whether the popup stops, the account is throttled or an overage is billed before you rely on the free tier for a launch.

Does PopupLoom include A/B testing like Popupsmart?

No. Popupsmart lists A/B split testing from its Advanced plan. PopupLoom has no split-testing feature, so you would compare versions by running them at different times or with your own analytics.

Can I migrate my Popupsmart popups and leads to PopupLoom?

There is no automatic importer. Export your Popupsmart submissions or connect your email provider before cancelling, then rebuild the live popup in PopupLoom and keep the old export as the historical record. New responses are stored in the PopupLoom lead inbox.
