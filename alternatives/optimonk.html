A free OptiMonk alternative with no pageview limits

OptiMonk terms checked on 14 November 2026. Verify current pricing on its site before buying.

OptiMonk is an ecommerce-focused popup platform with an AI agent, all features available on every plan and a genuinely useful free tier. It is a strong choice for a store that wants popups, A/B testing and analytics in one place. The free plan is still metered: 10,000 pageviews per month when we checked.

PopupLoom is free and does not meter pageviews. It is deliberately narrower: 45 templates, six triggers, page/device/frequency targeting, a lead inbox, CSV export and an optional signed webhook. It does not use an AI agent or include A/B testing.

Where OptiMonk is stronger

  • AI agent and all features on the free plan. Campaign creation, A/B testing and analytics are not gated by plan.
  • Ecommerce focus. Integrations and targeting built for stores.
  • Analytics and testing. A stronger experimentation workflow than PopupLoom.
  • More room before the cap. 10,000 pageviews per month is a generous free allowance for a small store.
  • No branding on any plan and a mature support operation.

Where PopupLoom fits

  • No pageview meter. Your popup does not pause because a monthly allowance ran out.
  • Any site with custom HTML. WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace, Wix or a static site.
  • Free with no card. No trial timer and no paid upgrade inside the product.
  • Portable leads. Lead inbox, CSV export and a signed server webhook.
  • 45 templates with honest setup notes for each category.

Who should switch

  • Your pageviews are near or above 10,000 per month.
  • You do not need an AI agent, A/B testing or ecommerce targeting.
  • You want a simple script and a focused popup tool.
  • You already use a separate email/CRM tool.

Who should stay

  • You want the AI agent and all features on the free plan.
  • You rely on A/B testing, analytics or store integrations.
  • You need a mature support organization.

Switch in about ten minutes

  1. Export your OptiMonk leads and keep the CSV.
  2. Build the popup in the PopupLoom studio and test it as a guest.
  3. Save it, open Publish, and copy the two-line install code.
  4. Add the code, pause the OptiMonk campaign, and test one fake submission.
  5. Confirm the response in the lead inbox, then remove the old campaign.

Platform steps: WordPress, Shopify, Webflow and Squarespace and Wix.

Common questions

Is PopupLoom a free OptiMonk alternative?

Yes. PopupLoom is free and does not meter pageviews. OptiMonk's free plan was listed at 10,000 pageviews per month and one domain when checked on 14 November 2026.

What is OptiMonk's free plan?

OptiMonk's pricing page on 14 November 2026 listed a free-forever plan with 10,000 pageviews per month, one domain, 1,000 AI credits and no credit card. At the pageview limit, campaigns pause until the counter resets or the account upgrades.

Does PopupLoom have an AI popup agent?

No. PopupLoom is a visual editor with 45 templates. It does not generate campaigns with an AI agent.

Does PopupLoom have A/B testing or geotargeting?

No. It has page, device and frequency targeting plus six triggers, but not A/B testing, geotargeting or audience segmentation.

Can I migrate my OptiMonk leads?

Export them from OptiMonk and keep the CSV. PopupLoom has no automatic importer; it collects new responses after installation.

Is PopupLoom affiliated with OptiMonk?

No. OptiMonk is a trademark of its owner. PopupLoom is not affiliated with or endorsed by OptiMonk.
