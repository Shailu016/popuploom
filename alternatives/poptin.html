A free Poptin alternative with no visitor limits

Poptin terms checked on 14 November 2026. Verify current pricing on its site before buying.

Poptin combines popups, forms and email tooling in one dashboard. Its free plan is one of the more generous entry points, but it is still metered: 1,000 unique visitors per month on one domain when we checked. If your traffic grows, the cap becomes the decision point.

PopupLoom is free and does not meter unique visitors or pageviews. It is a focused popup builder: 45 templates, six triggers, page/device/frequency targeting, a lead inbox, CSV export and an optional signed webhook. It does not send email or SMS.

Where Poptin is stronger

  • One dashboard for popups and email. Contacts, email campaigns and automations are part of the product.
  • Forms and popup types. A broad set of popup and form formats, templates and integrations.
  • Triggers and targeting. Exit intent, inactivity, cart triggers and advanced targeting on paid plans.
  • Support and integrations. A mature support operation and a wide integration list.

Where PopupLoom fits

  • No visitor cap. Your popup does not stop at 1,000 visitors or ask you to upgrade mid-month.
  • Free with no card. No trial timer and no paid upgrade inside the product.
  • Lightweight script. Two lines, no plugin, no app dependency.
  • Portable leads. Lead inbox, CSV export and a signed server webhook.
  • 45 templates with honest setup notes for each category.

Who should switch

  • You already use a separate email provider and do not need Poptin's email features.
  • Your traffic is near or above the 1,000-visitor free allowance.
  • You want a simple script and a focused popup tool.
  • You are comfortable exporting leads and handling follow-up elsewhere.

Who should stay

  • You want popups and email in one dashboard.
  • You rely on Poptin's advanced triggers, forms or integrations.
  • You need its support and managed onboarding.

Switch in about ten minutes

  1. Export your Poptin leads and keep the CSV.
  2. Build the popup in the PopupLoom studio and test it as a guest.
  3. Save it, open Publish, and copy the two-line install code.
  4. Add the code, pause the Poptin popup, and test one fake submission.
  5. Confirm the response in the lead inbox, then remove the old popup.

Platform steps: WordPress, Shopify, Webflow and Squarespace and Wix.

Common questions

Is PopupLoom a free Poptin alternative?

Yes. PopupLoom is free and does not meter unique visitors or pageviews. Poptin's free plan was listed at 1,000 unique visitors per month, one domain, when checked on 14 November 2026.

What is Poptin's free plan?

Poptin's pricing page on 14 November 2026 listed a free forever plan with 1,000 visitors per month and one domain. Verify the current terms on Poptin's pricing page.

Does PopupLoom include email sending?

No. PopupLoom captures the response and can export it or forward it to a signed webhook. Poptin's paid plans include email features; PopupLoom leaves email to your existing provider.

Can I use PopupLoom on Shopify?

Yes where the theme or an app allows custom HTML or JavaScript. See the Shopify guide for theme-code and app-embed options.

Can I migrate my Poptin leads?

Export them from Poptin and keep the CSV. PopupLoom has no automatic importer; it collects new responses after installation.

No. Poptin is a trademark of its owner. PopupLoom is not affiliated with or endorsed by Poptin.
