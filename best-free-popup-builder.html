Best free popup builders in 2026: an honest comparison

Vendor limits checked on 14 November 2026. Prices and limits change; verify on each linked pricing page.

“Best” depends on your platform, your traffic and the features you actually use. A WordPress-only plugin with unlimited impressions is not the same product as a script that works on Shopify, Webflow and a custom site. This page compares the free tiers we could verify and says which one fits which job.

The short answer

  • WordPress only, free, unlimited impressions: Popup Maker.
  • Any site, no pageview cap, simple script: PopupLoom.
  • Small store, AI agent and all features on free: OptiMonk (10K pageviews/month).
  • Popups plus email in one dashboard: Poptin (1,000 visitors/month).
  • Shopify app and AI builder: Popupsmart (5K pageviews/month).
  • Advanced paid campaign suite: OptinMonster (500-impression free trial).

How to choose in five questions

  1. What platform are you on? WordPress-only tools are not a fit for Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace, Wix or a custom app.
  2. What does the free tier meter? Pageviews, unique visitors, campaign impressions, live popups or contacts are different units.
  3. What happens at the cap? Stop, pause, auto-upgrade or overage charge.
  4. Which features are non-negotiable? A/B testing, geotargeting, cart data, email sending, SMS or a native app SDK.
  5. Who owns the lead? Check the export and webhook story before you collect a single address.

Why we did not rank them 1–6

A single ranking hides the trade-offs. Popup Maker is the best free option for many WordPress sites, but it cannot run on Shopify. OptiMonk has the most complete free feature set for a small store, but it still pauses at 10,000 pageviews. PopupLoom has no pageview cap, but it does not send email or run A/B tests. The right choice is the one that fits your platform and your must-have list.

What to verify before you commit

  • The free tier is permanent, not a trial.
  • The meter matches your traffic pattern.
  • Your platform can install the code.
  • You can export leads and cancel without losing data.
  • The pricing page is current and the limit is stated in the vendor's own words.

Common questions

What is the best free popup builder?

There is no single answer. Popup Maker is the strongest free WordPress-only option, OptiMonk has the most generous feature-complete free tier for small stores, and PopupLoom is the fit when you want no pageview cap and a script on any site. Choose by platform, meter and features, not by a generic ranking.

Which free popup builder has no pageview limit?

PopupLoom does not meter pageviews, visitors or impressions. Popup Maker's free WordPress plugin also advertises unlimited pageviews and impressions. OptiMonk, Poptin and Popupsmart publish monthly free-tier meters; OptinMonster's free offer is a 500-impression trial.

Is PopupLoom really free?

Yes. There is no credit card, trial timer or paid upgrade inside the product. A free account is required to save a popup and get its install code.

Do these free plans include email sending?

PopupLoom does not send email. Poptin and Popupsmart include email features with their own quotas. OptiMonk and OptinMonster focus on popups; email delivery is a separate tool.

They were checked on 14 November 2026. Free tiers change, so open each vendor's pricing page before you commit.
